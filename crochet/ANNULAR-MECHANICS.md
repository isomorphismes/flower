# One reduced mechanical realization of the synthetic annulus

This is a first stretching-and-bending model, separate from the exact analytic
benchmark in MATERIAL-GEOMETRY.md. It minimizes a declared finite energy; it is
not fitted to yarn and cannot represent ruffles or a general folded sheet.
The accepted stitch ledger and synthetic reference metric remain unchanged.

Four circular coaxial rings have radii r₀,…,r₃ and axial positions z₀,…,z₃.
Straight meridian segments connect them. Fix r₀ = R₀ and z₀ = 0; the other
six coordinates are free. Rᵢ denotes the reference metric's radius at row i,
h its supplied row spacing, and Aᵢ the exact reference area of band i.
For the exponential metric Aᵢ = 2π(Rᵢ₊₁ − Rᵢ)/k; for the cylinder it is 2πR₀h.
Each ring gets half the area of its adjacent bands as weight Wᵢ.

The segment length sᵢ is hypot(rᵢ₊₁ − rᵢ, zᵢ₊₁ − zᵢ). Its angle αᵢ is
atan2(zᵢ₊₁ − zᵢ, rᵢ₊₁ − rᵢ). The two modeled strains are sᵢ/h − 1 and rᵢ/Rᵢ − 1.
The hoop curvature of a conical band is (zᵢ₊₁ − zᵢ)/(sᵢ(rᵢ + rᵢ₊₁)/2).
At each interior ring use meridional curvature (αᵢ − αᵢ₋₁)/((sᵢ₋₁ + sᵢ)/2).

Define normalized stretching energy as one half the area-weighted sum of the
two squared strains, divided by total reference area A. Define normalized
bending energy as one half the corresponding weighted sum of squared hoop
and interior meridional curvatures, multiplied by t²/12 and divided by A.
Their sum is the minimized energy. Reference curvature is zero. This is our
specific discrete model with a zero Poisson-coupling convention, not a claim
that these quadrature choices constitute an established continuum yarn law.
The dimensional energy scale is YtA; positive modulus Y therefore rescales
energy without changing the unloaded minimizing shape. Thickness t controls
the relative bending weight. All current values are experiment parameters.

The inner ring's position is fixed but its tangent is free. The outer ring is
free. No gravity, imposed load, tension calibration, friction or external
contact is present. Radii stay positive and successive axial positions strictly
increase. This domain gives an injective zero-thickness surface of revolution:
different meridian positions lie in different axial planes. Reversed or
collapsed axial intervals are refused. Deliberately folded inputs are outside
this contact domain; no general collision detector is claimed. The t parameter
in the energy does not give the exported surface a volumetric thickness.
Finite-thickness self-contact and physical acceptance remain unsupported.

The six coordinates are normalized by 3h. A bounded BFGS iteration uses a
central finite-difference gradient with step 2×10⁻⁶, Armijo backtracking, and a
positive-curvature update. The default test uses tolerance 10⁻⁷ and at most
4,000 iterations. Exhaustion, a failed line search, or a gradient stencil
outside the admissible domain returns NONCONVERGED. A finite energy decrease
alone is not convergence. Invalid inputs leave the result unchanged; a
nonconverged result remains inspectable but cannot be exported as a solved
mesh. Writers recompute energy and gradient, so a forged success flag fails.

Tests compare two initial states, the no-increase cylinder, two thicknesses,
and a doubled length unit. The cylinder's initial stretching energy is zero;
its hoop bending has the independent closed value t²/(24R₀²). Perturbed radii,
outside-domain folds, exhausted iteration budgets, nonfinite values, forged
counts/calibration and forged convergence are separate controls. Independent
acceptance must recompute energy and stationarity from output coordinates,
not trust the native result flag or gradient. Native tests are not that oracle.

The output retains all 65 stitch IDs and typed parents. Density one and two
sample the same solved piecewise-linear meridian, not different mechanical
discretizations. The original smooth benchmark already supplies the separate
reference-refinement checks. This reduced solver has four mechanical rings;
it does not claim convergence to a general shell or a continuum plate equation.
Euclidean viewing and host/device/handmade acceptance remain separate.
